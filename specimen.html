01 · The weights

Weight comes in tiers, never a continuum

A rule this studio learned from cloth, not from software: in the kené textiles read for the IKARAO engagement, a line gets heavier in three or four fixed steps — each step a doubling, never a gradual thickening. The register works the same way. Raveo is a variable face that could render any of 800 weights; the house permits seven. Each row below is set at its own weight — the table is its own sample.

03 · The pair

Raveo speaks. The mono labels.

Two voices, with a hard division of labour: the display face carries meaning and is allowed feeling; the system mono carries data and is not. A number, a serial, a date, a caption, a check — if it must be exact, it is mono. If it must be believed, it is Raveo. Neither does the other’s job.

04 · The seed

Same words, same setting, forever

The same discipline that draws the site’s crests sets this specimen. Type anything: its weight, size and tracking are derived from the words themselves — the text is its own seed, hashed into the permitted tiers, never picked by mood. Retype it next year and the setting is identical.

The audit · measured 2026-08-26
The law says seven weights. The site, measured today, uses twenty-five.

A specimen that only showed the rules would be a style guide — and nobody audits a style guide, which is why they quietly stop being true. So this one publishes its own violation count. Seven weights carry two-thirds of the site’s type — 112 of 166 settings, counted; eighteen stragglers — one-off 480s, a lone 650, a 130 — are drift, on the ledger to be consolidated or promoted. When the count reaches seven, this paragraph gets shorter.
