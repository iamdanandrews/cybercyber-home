The work
01 / GRANITE

Granite is a strength coach that runs entirely on your phone. Not a plan you follow, but an engine that reasons — building your training around the life you actually train through, rewriting it each week as things change. We built it deterministic and on-device on purpose: your training history never leaves your hands, and every decision it makes can be traced, tested, and trusted.

A coach, not a plan

Most training apps hand you a fixed plan and hope you follow it. Granite works the other way round. It reasons — taking your goals, your experience, the days you actually have, your injuries, and the load you carry off the platform — and builds a week that fits your real life. When something changes, it rewrites, and tells you why. Not a plan you obey. A coach that adapts.

Fig. 01 · Granite · the check-inYou tell it what changed this week — a tweak, a bad night, a niggle — and the plan reasons around it.

The engine is the product

The intelligence in Granite is deterministic, not generative — and that’s a deliberate choice, not a limitation. It means every plan is consistent, every decision can be traced, and the whole system can be tested against real coaching principles rather than hoping a black box got it right. Accountable intelligence beats impressive intelligence when it’s your body on the line. We built the engine first, and everything else serves it.

On-device by decision

Granite runs entirely on your phone. No account, no cloud, no data leaving your hands — the privacy isn’t a policy you have to trust, it’s the architecture. Running locally was the harder path, and it made the product sharper: an engine that can’t lean on the cloud has to be deliberate about every decision. That constraint is the craft. Granite is the considered version of something most people build the easy way.

Fig. 02 · Granite · progressEvery session and every trend lives on the phone. No account, no cloud — nothing leaves your hands.
The ledger · kept / cut
The product is the decisions we refused to skip.

+Kept

  • A deterministic engine you can trace
  • On-device — no account, no cloud
  • A weekly rewrite that explains itself

−Cut

  • A generative black box guessing your plan
  • Cloud sync and sign-in
  • Streaks, badges, a feed to scroll
One we killed

We almost shipped cloud sync in week three — it would have made onboarding one tap and the demo look cleaner. We killed it because the moment your training history touches our servers, “on-device and accountable” stops being an architecture and becomes a marketing line. The harder path was the product.
