The work
02 / FRAME

The problem

What you drill on the mats disappears within days. A notebook doesn’t fight that — it just stores it, and storage isn’t memory. The technique you learned last week is already fading while you write it down.

The approach

Frame is built around recall, not storage — it surfaces what you’re about to forget, when it matters, instead of burying it in a log. A belt-accent identity engine ties the whole interface to where you actually are on the journey, so the app grows up with the practitioner.

The work

Designed and built as one native product: the fast post-session logging flow, the recall model that resurfaces technique, and the identity system that colours the app to your belt.
