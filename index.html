In short04 / QUESTIONS

Questions

How the work is scoped, shared and built.

What happens after launch?+

The handover, documentation and support period are agreed in the release scope. Continued improvements can be commissioned separately or through a Design Partnership.

What can I hire you for?+

A focused direction engagement, a scoped product release, or ongoing design and technical work with your team. The three engagement options and their starting prices are above.

How is delivery organised?+

I lead identity, interface and implementation together within an agreed scope. Your engineers can handle the build where that is the right arrangement. Specialist needs and larger programmes are discussed before an engagement is agreed.

What happens if the scope changes?+

We agree the scope and price before work starts. New requirements are scoped and quoted separately, so the trade-offs stay visible.

Can you work with our engineers?+

Yes. Product Direction and Partnership can focus on brand, interface and technical direction while your team handles the build.

How long does the work take?+

Product Direction runs for one or two weeks. Builds are scoped around the product and its requirements; the timeline is agreed with the scope.

How will I see progress?+

You work directly with me, and the work is shared as it develops. Design and implementation can be reviewed together rather than waiting for a final handover.

Who does the work?+

Dan Andrews, a designer in London with a decade across brand, product, motion and interaction, including work with Spotify, Epic Games and Dell. More about me ↗.
