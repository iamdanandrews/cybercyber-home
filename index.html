In short04 / QUESTIONS

Questions

The short version — what we are, what it costs, and who it's for.

What is cyber:cyber?+

cyber:cyber is a product design lab in London. Dan Andrews designs and builds products end to end — the brand, the interface, and the code underneath — for a few clients a year.

What does it do?+

Product design and build in one pair of hands: brand, interface, behaviour, and the production engineering underneath. Design that ships intact, with no handoff between design and code.

Who is it for?+

Founders who want the whole product shipped, not just designed. Engagements are fixed-scope and fixed-price, and deliberately few — a handful of clients a year.

How much does it cost?+

Fixed prices: from £5,000 / week for a Design Block, and £50,000+ for a complete build. The Partnership is from £12,000 / month.

Does it build AI products?+

Yes — native, on-device AI and agent features when the product genuinely needs them. But AI is a tool, not the identity; the craft is product design.

Who runs it?+

Dan Andrews, a designer and engineer in London with a decade of product design across consumer health, developer platforms, and coaching.
