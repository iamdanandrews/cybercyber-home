03 / Case study

The clinic’s overview.
The patient’s detail.

A clinical product connecting the caseload, rehabilitation plans and the detail of care. Product direction, information architecture and implementation.

Scope
Product direction · Interaction design · Web + iOS
Project
Owned product · Private pilot · Dan Andrews · 2026
The brief

A plan builder is only one part of running a clinic.

A physiotherapist moves between many patients and the detail of one recovery. Kaido gives those tasks different levels of information within a connected workspace. I designed and built the product around that movement, across web and native iOS.

The defining decision

Separate the clinic’s overview from the detail of a plan. Each level answers a different question, while the product connects them.

01 / DirectionKaido

An overview that leads somewhere.

A caseload row cannot carry the full clinical record. Its job is to establish context and provide a route into the relevant detail.

The constraint

An overview has to make many patients scannable. Giving every row the full rehabilitation record would obscure the clinician’s route into an individual patient.

The judgement

Use the caseload for context and navigation. Patient rehabilitation is a separate view, where exercises and criteria can take priority. The captured states show those levels, not a clinical outcome.

02 / ExperienceKaido

A starting structure for the plan.

The library makes protocols comparable before opening them. Body region, rehabilitation arc and criteria count establish a starting structure.

The constraint

Choosing a starting protocol and delivering an individual plan are different decisions. A library needs a comparable structure before the detailed exercise content is opened.

The judgement

Organise the starting library by body region, arc and criteria. Keep the complete protocol as its own workspace. The structure gives the clinician a route from selection to inspection.

03 / SystemKaido

Give the clinical detail room.

Exercises and research need different reading modes. The current Research tab separates the previous plan text, recommendation, detail and sources. That structure exposes the reasoning behind the protocol without filling the exercise overview with the entire appraisal.

The constraint

Research appraisal and exercise execution require different reading depths. Putting the complete appraisal beside every exercise would overload the overview.

The judgement

Separate Overview and Research. Sources and reasoning stay inspectable without taking over the exercise reading mode. The screenshots support the information hierarchy; clinical effectiveness remains unclaimed.

Delivered value

A connected clinic product ready to learn from use.

A working clinical workspace connecting caseload, plan library and protocol detail, with web and native iOS surfaces. Kaido is in a private clinic pilot.

Delivered capability

A working workspace connects caseload, condition library and protocol detail. Product direction, information architecture and implementation were delivered together.

Evidence stage

The product is in a private pilot. These examples use fictional patients; clinical outcomes, adoption and renewal figures are not claimed.
